## Intent

Premium cinematic product commercial for HueFlow (https://hueflow.site), a modern
color discovery and color system platform. User's brief, verbatim essentials:

Start with a warm off-white minimal background. A single vibrant color appears in the
center of the screen as a small glowing color tile. The tile smoothly expands into
multiple harmonious colors. Transition into the HueFlow interface: beautiful color
palettes, color combinations, gradients, color picker, color trends and color
exploration cards appear with elegant motion. Show a designer discovering a color,
expanding it into a complete palette, exploring complementary colors, and turning the
palette into a complete visual system.

Quick visual transformations: One color → Palette → Combination → System → Design.
End with a beautiful full-screen HueFlow interface. Display "One color. Infinite
possibilities." Then: HUEFLOW / Discover your next color.

Feel: premium SaaS commercial, Apple-inspired product presentation, modern design
studio aesthetic, cinematic lighting, ultra-clean UI, smooth 3D motion.
